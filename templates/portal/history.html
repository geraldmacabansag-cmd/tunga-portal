Our History

From a quiet barrio of Barugo to an independent municipality — the story of Tunga's growth and resilience.

The Founding of Tunga

The Municipality of Tunga was formerly a barrio of the Municipality of Barugo. Through Executive Order No. 266, signed by President Elpidio Quirino on September 26, 1949, Tunga was officially created as an independent municipality.

Since its creation, Tunga has continued to develop while preserving its agricultural heritage, close-knit community, and local traditions. Generations of Tunganons have built the municipality's institutions, from its first barangay councils to its modern local government offices.

  • 1949

    Executive Order No. 266 was signed on September 26, 1949, creating Tunga as an independent municipality.

  • 1950s

    Establishment of the municipal government and initial development of public services.

  • 1970s

    Expansion of infrastructure, schools, and health services in the municipality.

  • 1990s

    Growth of agriculture and local industries, improving the livelihood of residents.

  • 2000s

    Strengthening of governance and community participation in local development.

  • Present

    Tunga continues to progress towards a more resilient, inclusive, and sustainable future.
