Business Permit Application

Published

This service allows individuals or businesses to apply for a business permit to operate legally within the Municipality of Tunga.

Last updated: May 16, 2026 2:45 PM
Service Process Requirements Fees Office Information Settings

Process Steps

1
Get Application Form
Obtain the Business Permit Application Form from the Mayor's Office or download it online.
Form Available
2
Prepare Requirements
Prepare and secure all the required documents needed for the application.
3
Submit Application
Submit the accomplished application form along with the complete requirements to the Mayor's Office.
4
Assessment and Evaluation
The Mayor's Office will assess and evaluate your application and documents.
Pay Fees
5
Approval
Once approved, the Business Permit will be issued.
6
Release of Business Permit
Claim your Business Permit at the Mayor's Office.

Important Reminders

  • Make sure all documents are complete and updated.
  • Applications with incomplete requirements will not be accepted.
  • Processing time may vary depending on the nature of the business.
